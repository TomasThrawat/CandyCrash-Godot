# Asset and audio notes

## SFXMint

The following sound roles were selected from the SFXMint puzzle-game set:

- match
- slide
- combo
- bonus
- celebration
- win
- lose
- countdown

SFXMint reported the set license as CC0-1.0.

## Procedural game art

Candy sprites, board cells, particles, buttons, and the background scene are generated by the Godot scripts in this repository.

## Background music

assets/audio/music.wav is generated locally with a small procedural melody and has no external sample dependency.
